Scatter

How to play

Some of the stones are blue. Find them all. A number on a stone counts the blue stones inside its circle. Hover over a number (or tap it) to see its circle.

This 3 already has 3 blue stones inside its circle, so the grey stone inside it must be dark.

Click a stone to mark it blue; right-click (or long-press) to mark it dark. Each wrong mark costs a point. Once a number has all its blues, click it to open the rest of its circle. The board is one long strip: faint stones to the right open as you get through the part before them, and it gets harder as you go. Collect the coins hidden under blue stones on the way; solve the last section to finish the run. Puzzles come in levels of three and get harder as you go. Solve all three in a level to earn a hint.

How to play
  • Find every blue stone. Left-click marks a stone blue, right-click marks it dark. On touch screens, the mode button sets what a tap does.
  • A number on a revealed stone counts the blue stones inside its circle, not counting itself. Circles never cross a stone, so every stone is clearly in or out.
  • Hover over a number to see its circle. Every clue has its own circle size. Shift-click a number to pin its circle; right-click it to grey it out once you have used it.
  • Chording: once you have found all the blues in a circle, click its number to open every other stone in it as dark. If you are wrong, it costs a point and nothing opens.
  • A revealed stone without a number gives no information.
  • Nothing tells you how many blue stones are left. You have to count.
  • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. A wrong click reveals nothing.
  • Points: you start with 10 (Easy), 5 (Normal) or 2 (Hard). Each wrong click costs one; at zero the game ends. Finish with as many left as you can.
  • Hints: you get 2 per session. Solve 3 puzzles in a row to earn another. Running out of points or starting a new puzzle before finishing resets the streak.
  • XL: a stone whose circle holds no blue at all has no number; finding it shades its circle and opens every stone in it by itself. Any such stone it uncovers opens its circle too.
  • Progress: a finished circle (every stone in it revealed) leaves a faint ring behind, and soft music builds as more circles are finished. The bar shows how much of the board is revealed. A few blue stones hold a coin, which appears when you reveal the stone yourself.
